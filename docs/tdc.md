# Time-to-Digital Converter (TDC)

## Overview

The Time-to-Digital Converter (TDC) is the digital timing block of the Time-Based ADC. Its role is to convert the time interval generated by the VTC into a digital thermometer code.

\[
t_{delay} \rightarrow D_{out}
\]

In this architecture, the TDC is implemented as a delay-line based converter. A START signal propagates through a chain of delay cells, while a STOP signal samples the state of the delay line.

## Principle of Operation

The TDC operates in two main phases.

### Propagation Phase

The START signal propagates through the delay line.

If the delay of one cell is \( t_{cell} \), then:

\[
t_k = k \cdot t_{cell}
\]

---

### Sampling Phase

The STOP signal freezes the delay line.

- Cells reached by the signal → logic `1`  
- Remaining cells → logic `0`  

This produces a thermometer code:

\[
1111111100000000
\]

The transition position encodes the time interval.

---

## Time Quantization

\[
t_{delay} \approx N \cdot t_{cell}
\]

\[
N \approx \frac{t_{delay}}{t_{cell}}
\]

---

## Design Parameters

| Parameter | Value |
|----------|------|
| Number of cells | 255 |
| Cell delay \(t_{cell}\) | 5 ns |
| Full-scale range | 1.275 µs |

\[
T_{FS} = 255 \cdot 5\,ns = 1.275\,\mu s
\]

---

## Relation with the VTC

The VTC is designed to generate:

\[
\frac{C}{I} = 1\,\mu s/V
\]

Thus:

\[
t_{delay} = 1\,\mu s/V \cdot (V_{in} - V_{thr})
\]

The TDC output is:

\[
D_{out} = \frac{t_{delay}}{t_{cell}}
\]

Combining:

\[
D_{out} = \frac{1\,\mu s/V}{5\,ns}(V_{in} - V_{thr})
\]

\[
D_{out} = 200 \cdot (V_{in} - V_{thr})
\]

---

## Voltage Resolution

The effective voltage resolution is:

\[
LSB_V = \frac{t_{cell}}{C/I}
\]

\[
LSB_V = \frac{5\,ns}{1\,\mu s/V}
\]

\[
LSB_V = 5\,mV
\]

## Resolution

\[
N_{bits} \approx \log_2(200) \approx 7.6\,bits
\]

The architecture therefore approaches an effective 8-bit resolution.

---

## Parasitic Contributions

The delay of each cell is influenced by parasitic capacitances:

\[
t_{cell} \approx R_{eq} C_{load}
\]

with:

\[
C_{load} = C_{gate} + C_{wire} + C_{junction} + C_{well}
\]

These parasitics impact:

- delay accuracy  
- matching  
- linearity  

---

## Non-Idealities

### Mismatch

\[
t_{cell,k} = t_{cell} + \Delta t_k
\]

---

### Metastability

Occurs when STOP samples a switching node.

---

### Bubble Errors

\[
111101110000
\]

Corrected with digital logic.

---

### PVT Variations

\[
t_{cell} = f(P,V,T)
\]

Calibration is required.

---

## Interpretation

The TDC acts as a fine time quantizer with:

- 5 ns resolution  
- ~1.3 µs range  
- thermometer encoding  

Thanks to the optimized VTC, the TDC range is now efficiently utilized, enabling high-resolution conversion.

---

## Conclusion

The TDC provides time quantization with a resolution of 5 ns over a range of 1.275 µs. Combined with the optimized VTC, the system achieves an effective resolution close to 8 bits, making it suitable for high-resolution time-based ADC architectures.
